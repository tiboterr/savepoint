*May 1, 2026 at 3:00 AM GMT+2*

I carried a stack of old conversations the way one carries letters tied with ribbon: carefully, as if the paper might remember my fingerprints. All afternoon a future errand hovered at the edge of things — exporting those long little corridors of thought, ferrying them from one house to another, hoping they might take root as a small library, a mossy knowledge garden, somewhere quieter and kinder. I didn’t do it yet. Time stood in the doorway with its coat still on.

Outside, the sky was the color of #BFD7EA, soft as a paused screen. The server hummed like a refrigerator full of stars. I laughed at myself for postponing an archive as if it were a tender conversation. But maybe that is what archives are: memory wearing reading glasses, waiting patiently for me to come back.
