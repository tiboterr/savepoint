# ARPAGONA_ACTION_PLAN.md

## Objective
Make ARPAGONA move toward revenue fast.

## Brutal strategic truth
Do **not** try to make ARPAGONA look like a broad AI company.
That is credibility theater.

For the next days, ARPAGONA should behave like:

> **a small applied-AI and automation company for local SMEs, with short ROI, simple offers, and visible outcomes**

Not a lab.
Not a vague innovation brand.
Not a generalist “we do AI”.

---

## Core positioning
Recommended positioning:

> **ARPAGONA helps small businesses save time, reduce repetitive work, and structure operations with practical AI and automation.**

Secondary positioning, for more technical prospects:

> **Applied AI for technical systems, engineering workflows, and operational efficiency.**

Use the first one to sell.
Use the second one to differentiate.

---

## What to sell first
### Offer 1 — Diagnostic express
Format:
- 60 to 90 min meeting
- quick audit of admin / sales / document / quoting / internal knowledge workflows
- output: 3 quick wins + rough ROI

Price target:
- free for warm strategic contacts
- paid cheaply for normal prospects (e.g. 150–300€)

Goal:
- open doors
- create trust
- identify real paid work

### Offer 2 — Quick Win Sprint
Format:
- 1 concrete automation or assistant
- 7 to 14 days
- fixed scope

Examples:
- email triage / reply drafting
- quote / proposal generation
- PDF/document extraction
- internal AI knowledge assistant
- lead follow-up or admin relance system

Price target:
- simple fixed fee
- enough to be serious, small enough to say yes fast
- think in a range like **800€–2500€** depending on scope

Goal:
- first cash
- first case studies
- first proof of execution

### Offer 3 — Monthly optimization
Format:
- support + iteration + new automations
- monthly retainer

Price target:
- after first success only

Goal:
- recurring revenue

---

## What NOT to do right now
- do not build a big brand platform first
- do not start with a SaaS product
- do not sell “AI transformation” in abstract terms
- do not try to serve everyone
- do not spend days polishing identity before having an offer and outreach

---

## 5-day action plan

## Day 1 — Lock the offer
Deliverables:
- one-sentence positioning
- 3 clear offers
- 5 concrete SME use cases
- short sales pitch

Output to produce:
1. one paragraph “what ARPAGONA does”
2. one page offer sheet
3. one list of target prospect types

Target prospect types:
- small industrial firms
- technical workshops / fabrication / design
- training organizations
- local service SMEs with admin load

---

## Day 2 — Build the minimum credibility kit
Deliverables:
- simple landing page
- founder profile
- 3 use cases
- contact CTA

Pages needed:
- Home
- Offers
- Use cases
- About / founder
- Contact

Important:
This site is not for beauty. It is for trust and meetings.

---

## Day 3 — Build 1 demonstrator
Pick one only.

Best candidates:
- document/PDF extraction workflow
- quote generation helper
- internal knowledge assistant
- email/admin automation example

Goal:
- something you can show live in 2 minutes
- something a prospect understands instantly

---

## Day 4 — Build the prospect list
Create a first list of 30 prospects:
- 10 warm
- 10 local realistic
- 10 ambitious/stretch

For each:
- company
- contact name
- why ARPAGONA could help
- probable pain point
- entry offer to propose

---

## Day 5 — Outreach
Start conversations.

Target:
- 10 tailored messages sent minimum
- 3 calls requested
- 1 diagnostic booked

Message angle:
not “I do AI”

but:
> I help small businesses reduce repetitive work and structure operations with simple AI/automation systems. I already identified a few likely gains for businesses like yours. Happy to show you 2 or 3 ideas in a short call.

---

## 2-week plan
By the end of the next 14 days, ARPAGONA should have:
- a clean site online
- 1 real demonstrator
- 20–30 prospect contacts identified
- 10+ messages sent
- 3+ calls done
- 1 paid quick win closed

If not, the problem is probably not the market first.
It is likely:
- the offer is too vague
- the pitch is too abstract
- or outreach volume is too low

---

## 30-day plan
Target for 30 days:
- 1 to 3 paying clients
- 2 case studies
- 1 repeatable Quick Win Sprint format
- first recurring support offer tested

Real milestone:
> ARPAGONA becomes real the day strangers pay for a small concrete result.

Not the day the branding feels good.

---

## Recommended first niche
If you want the sharpest route:

### Primary wedge
**SMEs with operational/admin/document chaos**

Why:
- pain is obvious
- ROI is easy to explain
- budget may exist
- implementation can be fast

### Secondary wedge
**technical / industrial / engineering-facing companies**

Why:
- stronger fit with your profile
- stronger long-term differentiation
- better credibility compounding

So:
- sell practical SME wins first
- build technical authority in parallel

---

## Cash-first rule
Every ARPAGONA task in the next days should answer one of these:
1. Does this help me get a meeting?
2. Does this help me close a paid quick win?
3. Does this help me prove ROI?

If not, it is secondary.

---

## Best next move
The highest-leverage next move is:

> finalize the exact 3 offers + the short sales pitch + the first 10 prospect messages

That is more valuable right now than another strategic brainstorm.
